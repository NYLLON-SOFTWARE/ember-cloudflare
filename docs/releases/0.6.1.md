Ember 0.6.1 gives Ember a flat icon: its flame's outline in a sunset's colors on white, or in brighter ones on near-black where your system is dark, in place of 0.6.0's white flame on a sunset. The browser's tab shows it in each, in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, and Safari shows it light, as do a home screen and an installed app, which no browser switches yet. While a workspace has a logo, the tab shows the logo, as before.

## Updating

Your copy's Update Ember workflow offers it as a pull request, "Update Ember to v0.6.1", at its next weekly run, or now if you run it from the Actions tab. From 0.6.0, merging it deploys the new icon and nothing else: it changes no table, binding or secret. A copy on 0.5 or 0.6 beta 1 is offered 0.6.1 in place of 0.6.0, and updating it does what [0.6.0's notes](https://github.com/NYLLON-SOFTWARE/ember-cloudflare/releases/tag/v0.6.0) say as well.
